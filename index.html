👁️ Use the camera to follow the eyes?

Talk Board can use the iPad's front camera to see where you are looking, so words can be picked with the eyes alone.

  • The video stays on this iPad. It is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.
  • The camera only runs while Talk Board is open.
  • You can turn it off at any time in caregiver settings.

On the next screen the iPad will ask for permission. Choose Allow.

Eye tracking setup

  1. Make sure the whole face is in the picture, with good light on it.
  2. Keep the iPad still, about an arm's length away.
  3. Look at 5 dots, one at a time. Keep the head still and move only the eyes.
